Research focus

Understanding a changing polar climate

Why does polar sea ice vary—and what drives extreme loss?

I investigate the mechanisms controlling sea ice behaviour and its predictability from seasonal to decadal timescales.

My research combines large climate model ensembles, satellite observations, and statistical methods to separate the effects of internal climate variability from longer-term change.

By identifying the atmospheric and oceanic drivers behind extreme events, I aim to improve understanding of polar predictability and how sea ice change influences the wider global climate system.

Featured video · Climate change

A changing climate—at an unprecedented pace

Earth’s climate has always changed. We have experienced Ice Ages with giant ice sheets and warm, tropical worlds with no ice at all—but those shifts unfolded over tens of thousands to millions of years.

Today, humanity has warmed the planet by more than 1 °C in under 200 years. This video explores why the pace of modern climate change matters.

Science & policy

UK Parliament · POST placement

Turning evidence into policy insight.

I took a break from my PhD to undertake a policy placement with the Parliamentary Office of Science and Technology at the UK Parliament.

At POST, I worked on a parliamentary briefing examining the risks, management, and policy challenges associated with landfills in the UK.

The work involved synthesising evidence across environmental science, regulation, and policy, and engaging with academics, regulators, local authorities, and other stakeholders to understand scientific uncertainties and real-world decision-making challenges around landfill contamination.

04 · Beyond research

Science, community
and communication.

Before starting my PhD, I completed an MSc in Oceanography at the University of Southampton, analysing animal-tracking data from marine predators to understand foraging distributions and environmental drivers of habitat use.

This work introduced me to polar ecology and developed my interest in how physical ocean processes interact with marine wildlife. Earlier in my career, I worked as an Assistant Transport Planner at Transport Planning Associates from 2018 to 2021.

I serve as the Early Career Researcher Representative for AntClimNow and Social Media Officer for the UK Polar Network, helping to support and connect early-career polar scientists globally. I also contribute to teaching natural sciences and climate modules at Exeter.
